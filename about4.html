Key Aspects of Graduation

Recognition of Achievement
It serves as formal, public validation that a student has earned their degree, diploma, or certificate.
The Ceremony (Commencement)
Students often wear academic gowns and caps, participating in traditions like the procession and receiving diplomas. While graduation means finishing, the ceremony is often called"commencement" to symbolize the start of a new chapter.
Sign of Transition
It marks the end of a student's academic journey and the beginning of a professional life, higher education, or new responsibilities.
